Each cluster serves two roles in the system:
① Importance weight — fed into XGBoost as a feature. High-traffic clusters get a higher weight, so the model prioritises their patterns when learning to forecast.
② Refill threshold — the cash % that triggers a replenishment trip. Derived from each cluster's average daily drain rate (velocity) × service cycle + safety buffer. Higher-drain clusters trigger earlier.
